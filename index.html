Pike & Shot Bots

Raise two 17th-century armies on a budget. Every company costs its kit up front, then wages and food for each day in the field: horse eat far more than foot. Give each company a captain's personality, pick the ground and the weather, then watch them fight it out, or run a hundred battles.

Bars under each company:  strength  (top) and  morale  (green → amber shaken → red breaking). Ragged ranks = disordered; scattered figures = running.

Historical armies, each bought to the same budget, fight a round robin. Sides swap every other battle and the ground is mirrored, so only the order of battle differs.

What is a pound best spent on? Each army is a single company type, bought to the same budget, and they all fight each other. Cheap men come in crowds; dear horse come in handfuls.

Same army, different captains. A personality is a command style, not a stat: it decides when to fire, when to charge, whether to stand by the pikes and whether to chase the beaten off the field.

Captains' tournament

Every captain (presets and yours) commands every company of the same army; each pairing is fought on mirrored ground.

Captain editor
